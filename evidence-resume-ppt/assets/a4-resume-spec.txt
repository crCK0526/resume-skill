DEFAULT A4 SINGLE-PAGE RESUME SPECIFICATION

Canvas
- Portrait A4: 21.0 cm × 29.7 cm
- One slide only
- Background: #FFFFFF or #FAFBFD
- Safe margin: 1.4-1.7 cm

Typography
- Chinese font: Microsoft YaHei or another confirmed installed sans-serif
- Page label: 11-13 pt
- Candidate name: 20-26 pt when used as primary heading
- Section title: 11-13 pt, bold
- Entry title: 10-11 pt, bold
- Body: 9.5-10.5 pt
- Dates: 9.5-10.5 pt, right aligned

Color system
- Primary blue: #0070C0
- Dark blue: #1F3B63
- Light blue: #8EAADB
- Main text: #1E1E1E
- Secondary text: #595959

Layout
- Preserve the reference resume's hierarchy when provided.
- Prefer a compact two-column basic-information block followed by a single-column content area.
- Keep section dividers consistent in length and weight.
- Use a portrait placeholder only when no approved photo exists.
- Avoid skill-rating dots, progress bars, or decorative sidebars unless requested or evidenced.

Quality thresholds
- No body text below 9 pt.
- No text overflow or clipped lines.
- No unresolved placeholder in a final version.
- No full-page image used as the editable resume body.
- Keep all substantive text and shapes editable.
